Tim was fortunate to be awarded $2.8 million dollars for a new project *Scalable Human Augmentation with Deference and Explainability*, awarded by the Advanced Strategic Capabilities Accelerator (ASCA). SHADE is one of handful of projects announced as part of ASCA's Decision Advantage program.

Tim will lead the project, with CIs A/Prof Pier Howe from the University of Melbourne, and Dr Leon Clark from Lockheed Martin Australia.

The project will investigate how to make decisions *at scale*, experimenting with techniques such as 'learning to defer', and aiming to pioneer new project in the area of 'explainability sets' -- grouping and explaining sets of decisions to reduce cognitive load.

Stay tuned for more once the project beings in February!
