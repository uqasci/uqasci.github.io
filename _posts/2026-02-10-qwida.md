Tim is one of 18 chief investigators part of a new $94.6 million ARC Centre of Excellence to empower workers in the digital age.

The ARC Centre of Excellence for Quality Work in a Digital Age (QWiDA) will work with international researchers, industry, and government on how workers and organisations can better use technology in the workplace. The UQ node will lead research on augmenting working with artificial intelligence and automation, looking at effects beyond just technology efficiency, such as the effect on work quality, worker well-being, and investigating important topics such as skill erosion from artificial intelligence.

You can read more about it at the [QWiDA webpage](https://www.qwida.org.au/)
